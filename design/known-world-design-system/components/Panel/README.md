# Panel

The raised surface of the board: `panel` fill, a `line` hairline and `radius-md` corners, with no shadow.

- `.kw-panel` holds the stage, the legend and the anatomy figures. Its padding is `space-12`.
- Inside a panel, the frame or a drawing sits on `.kw-panel--canvas` (the `canvas` colour, `radius-sm`), so it reads as a screen.
- Captions under a figure use `kw-small` in `fg-2`, with the subject in bold.
