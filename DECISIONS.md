## Remaining trade-offs

- Relationship dates rest on manifest commits; ten of twelve are one batch, drawn as candidates until a MIRV or session
  touches both ends.
- Drive-to-machine assignment before Linux is unknown and stays untinted.
- Regime marks confirmed with a lag are later than the change itself; the alternative leaks the future.
- Chords cap at four heads with "+n".
- Session-to-lineage joins by path prefix for 226 AI sessions; a wrong prefix would mis-tint a region (checked in the
  LAP-15 receipt by sampling).
- The look-ahead camera reads smoother; it is the arm, not the default.

## Rejected alternatives

- Machines-as-eras continents (OMEN-Windows, AM4-Windows, ...): not evidenced by the corpus.
- Per-commit flashes: 32,121 flashes in nine minutes.
- Time-window grouping as the default: proximity guesses an engagement; a shared session observes it (kept as the control
  arm).
- A left panel: the quiet edge is the medium.
- Explanatory captions or permanent labels: behaviour must carry the story; names enrich, never explain.
- Islands joining the camera box: they pulled the land off centre.
- Reflowing the nation panel as nations enter: a layout shift is not an event.
